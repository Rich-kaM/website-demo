Digital infrastructure and public-sector technology

Oguzu Lee Denis

Technology and Governance Adviser

A Ugandan Member of Parliament for Maracha County, technology innovator, and champion for governance reform. Recognized by the World Health Organization for designing a national emergency ambulance service framework, he brings an ICT background to digital infrastructure expansion, illicit financial flows and youth-inclusive development.

Tariff analysis and electricity market regulation

Christian Bakole Mukulu

Energy Economist and Deputy Director of Economic and Pricing Affairs, ARE

A Congolese energy economist and Deputy Director of Economic and Pricing Affairs at the Democratic Republic of the Congo’s Electricity Regulatory Authority (ARE). A former Hubert H. Humphrey and Mandela Washington Fellow at UC Davis, he holds a Master’s degree in Energy Science and Policy and completed specialized training with the Korea Energy Agency. Supported by World Bank funding as a senior expert on electricity tariff analysis and market regulation, he promotes household-level energy efficiency and sustainable energy transition strategies across the DRC.

Let's Talk Together
Regional power markets and energy regulation

Dr. Geoffrey Aori Mabea

Energy Economist and CEO, RAERESA/COMESA

A Kenyan energy economist, author and executive serving as CEO of the Regional Association of Energy Regulators for Eastern and Southern Africa (RAERESA/COMESA). Holding a PhD in Energy Economics from the University of Dundee, he brings leadership experience from Kenya’s geothermal sector, PwC and EREA, including work on the East African Community Energy Union and the Energy Regulation Centre of Excellence for cross-border power market integration, tariff reform and policy harmonization.

Let's Talk Together
National energy planning and public policy

Matthieu Abena Gongo

Energy Expert and Public Policy Adviser, CATE Coordinator

A Congolese energy expert and public policy adviser with technical and strategic leadership experience in the Democratic Republic of the Congo’s power sector. As Coordinator of the Technical and Economic Support Unit (CATE), he leads planning, economic analysis and validation of national energy projects, building on prior experience as Chief of Staff at the Ministry of Energy and Hydraulic Resources and chair of the steering committee for the Busanga Hydroelectric Power Station.
