# Pre-launch checklist

Company data

Confirm official telephone, official email, domain, legal registration details, tax details where needed, legal representative, privacy contact, verified social accounts, actual office hours, newsletter frequency, recruitment retention period and approved legal text.

People and images

Confirm exact public expert titles, French and English bios, photograph ownership, publication rights and written consent. Confirm hero-photo license if a photo-led hero is preferred.

Technical

Configure production secrets, SMTP, HTTPS, reverse proxy, persistent database, private upload storage, malware scanning, CAPTCHA if approved, backups, restore testing, monitoring and incident contacts.

QA

Run page inventory checks, 404 check, link crawl, Playwright overflow tests, browser tests, accessibility scan, dependency scan, secret scan, security headers review, production-build test and exact deployed-site verification.

Legal

Complete qualified legal review of Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Terms, Consent Management, Accessibility Statement, Legal Notices and Recruitment Privacy Notice.
